FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need prior trekking experience?

Not at all. Around 60% of our trekkers are first-timers. We rate every trek by difficulty and offer beginner-friendly options with full guidance.

What’s included in the price?

Base-camp-to-base-camp: accommodation, meals on the trek, certified guides, permits, safety equipment and first aid. Travel to the base is usually separate — check each trek page.

Is it safe for solo women travellers?

Absolutely. We have female leaders, vetted groups, a buddy finder and strict safety protocols. Many of our solo women trekkers become regulars.

What’s your refund & weather policy?

Weather-based cancellations by us are fully refundable or transferable. Personal cancellations follow a tiered policy shared at booking. Safety always comes first.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards, net-banking and EMI. Groups of five or more get an extra discount. Loyalty points and reward codes apply at checkout.
