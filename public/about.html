Our story

We turn first-timers into mountaineers

Born in Kolkata in 2019, Summit Sage exists to make the Himalayas accessible, safe and unforgettable — for everyone.

Our mission

Big mountains, warm people

It started with six friends, one rented tent and a shared belief: that the transformation you feel on a summit shouldn’t be reserved for the elite few. So we built a community that holds your hand on your first trek and challenges you on your fiftieth.

Today we’ve guided 8,000+ trekkers across 30+ Himalayan routes — with an obsessive focus on safety, sustainability and the little details that turn a trip into a story.

  • Zero-compromise safety
  • Leave-no-trace ethos
  • Community over crowds
  • Local guides & economies

The journey

How we grew, summit by summit

2019

Six friends, one dream

Summit Sage begins with a single Sandakphu trek and a WhatsApp group of 40 people.

2021

1,000 trekkers strong

We formalise safety protocols, onboard certified leaders and cross our first thousand summiteers.

2023

Rewards & community

Loyalty tiers, badges and the buddy finder launch — turning trekkers into a genuine family.

2024

Corporate & beyond

We expand into corporate retreats and cross 30 Himalayan routes with an 8,000-member community.

2026

The next ascent

New expeditions, deeper sustainability and a mission to reach 25,000 trekkers. You’re invited.
